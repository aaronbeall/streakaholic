Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The short version: the Streakaholic app doesn't collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Every habit, streak, and completion is stored only on your device. There's no account, no sign-in, and no server involved. The only personal information we ever hold is an email address you choose to give us, for example by joining the early access list on this website. The website itself uses Google Analytics to count visits.

This policy explains how the Streakaholic app for Android ("Streakaholic", "the app") and this website, streakaholic.app ("the website"), handle information. Streakaholic is made by Meta Modern Monkey ("we", "us"). If you have a question about anything here, email support@streakaholic.app.

Information the app collects

None. Streakaholic has no account system and no server of its own. The app doesn't include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools, and it doesn't send your habits or usage to us or to anyone else. We can't see what you track.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • The habits you create: names, icons, colors, schedules, reminder settings, and whether a habit uses Ironman mode.
  • Your completions: which days you checked off and when.
  • Streak stats, achievements, and Trophy Case history.
  • App preferences, such as theme, sound effects, and which tips you've already seen.

This information stays on your device. Other apps can't read it. It's removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage (see Delete your data).

Android device backup

If you've turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's data in the backup it saves to your Google account, so it can be restored to a new phone. That backup is run by Google under your Google account settings and Google's privacy policy. We never receive a copy.

Notifications

If you set reminders, the app asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder information leaves your device.

Export, import, and sharing

  • Export creates a backup file on your device. It only leaves your device if you choose to save or send it somewhere.
  • Import reads a file you pick. The app doesn't upload it anywhere.
  • Share cards are images made on your device. When you share one, it goes to the app you pick in Android's share sheet, and that app's own privacy policy applies from there.

Optional tips

The app includes an optional tip jar. Tips are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and shares only the purchase confirmation the app needs to say thank you. We never receive your name, email, or payment details. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles purchases.

Ratings and reviews

The app may occasionally offer to let you rate it. If you choose to, the rating screen is provided by Google Play, and anything you submit there is handled by Google.

This website

Early access list

If you join the early access list, your email address is collected and stored by EmailOctopus, the email service we use to run the list. We use it only to email you about Streakaholic, such as when testing opens or the app launches. You may be asked to confirm your address before you're added. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove you at any time. EmailOctopus handles your address under its privacy policy.

Hosting and fonts

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web server, they may log technical details such as your IP address and browser to deliver and secure the page, under GitHub's and Google's privacy policies.

Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects details such as the pages you view, how you arrived, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We use these reports only in aggregate, to improve the website, and we don't use them for advertising. Google handles this data under How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can block it with your browser's cookie or tracker settings, or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. This applies to the website only. The app has no analytics.

Data sharing and sale

We don't sell or rent your information. Your email address is shared only with EmailOctopus, and only to send you the emails you signed up for. Website usage data goes to Google Analytics as described above.

Children

Streakaholic isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has joined the early access list, email us and we'll remove them.

Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by your device's own security, such as your screen lock. Exported files aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Your choices

To leave the early access list, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or email us. You're in control of everything the app stores. You can edit or delete any habit in the app, export a copy at any time, and remove all of it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your data page.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app or website handles information changes, for example if a future version adds optional sync, we'll update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
