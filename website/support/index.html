Common questions

How do streaks work with different schedules?

A streak counts days in a row where the habit was on track. Days that aren't due, like a rest day on a habit you do Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, don't break it. For weekly or monthly goals, your streak keeps going as long as you're meeting the goal for that week or month. Tap the status badge on any habit to see a plain-English summary of where it stands.

Can I fix a day I forgot to check off?

Yes, for regular habits. Open the habit, go to Calendar, and tap the day to mark it done. Ironman habits are the exception: past days are locked on purpose.

What is Ironman mode?

Ironman is an optional setting for when you create a habit. Only today's check-in counts: past days lock and skips are off, so every streak is earned day by day. Ironman habits wear a badge and can earn Ironman-only achievements. Once a habit is Ironman, it can't be turned off.

My reminders aren't showing up.

Check these on your phone:

  • Notifications are allowed for Streakaholic (Settings → Apps → Streakaholic → Notifications).
  • Battery optimization isn't restricting the app. Set its battery use to Unrestricted if your phone offers it.
  • Do Not Disturb isn't on at the reminder time.

Reminders also skip habits that are already done for the day.

How do I move my habits to a new phone?

On your old phone, go to Settings → Export data and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, like a cloud drive or email. On the new phone, install Streakaholic and use Settings → Import data to pick that file.

Is my data synced or backed up online?

No. Streakaholic keeps everything on your device and has no account or server. Export a backup from time to time so you don't lose your history if something happens to your phone. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I turn off sounds or celebrations?

Go to Settings and turn off Sound effects or Celebrate achievements. You can also snooze a single kind of celebration with the bell button on its celebration screen.
