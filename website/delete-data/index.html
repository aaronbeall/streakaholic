Delete your data

Streakaholic by Meta Modern Monkey

Streakaholic has no accounts and keeps no data on any server. Everything the app stores is on your own device, so you can delete all of it yourself at any time, without asking us.

Delete everything

Either of these permanently removes all habits, completions, streaks, achievements, and settings. It can't be undone, so export a backup first if you might want it later.

Option 1: clear the app's storage

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and choose Streakaholic.
  3. Tap Storage, then Clear storage (sometimes called Clear data).

The app stays installed and starts fresh the next time you open it.

Option 2: uninstall the app

Uninstalling Streakaholic removes all of its data from your device.

Delete a single habit

  1. Open the habit and tap the edit (pencil) button.
  2. Tap Delete habit at the bottom of the form, then confirm.

The habit and all of its completions are removed. If you'd rather keep its history out of sight, choose Archive instead.

Other copies you may have made

  • Exported backup files are ordinary files. Delete them from wherever you saved them, such as your Downloads folder or a cloud drive.
  • Android device backup: if you use Android's backup to your Google account, a copy of the app's data may be in that backup. You can manage or delete it in your phone's backup settings or at Google Drive backups.
  • Tips: records of any tips are kept by Google Play under your Google account, not by us. See your Google account to manage them.

What we keep

Nothing. We don't receive or store any of your Streakaholic data, so there's nothing on our side to delete. If you've emailed us for support, you can ask us to delete that email conversation, and we'll do so within 30 days.
